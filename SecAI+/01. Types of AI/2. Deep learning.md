# Deep learning

- A specialised aspect of ML in which neural networks are used to process data.
- DL models learn patterns through multiple layers of processing.
- Deep learning can be used to detect subtle patterns in data.
  - Byte level patterns in malware.
- Transformer architecture of deep learning allows models to process large amounts of sequential data in parallel and to capture long-range relationships in text.
- Transformer architecture is modular, meaning it can grow to large sizes and process multi-modal data such as text, audio and video more effectively than older methods.
- Transformer architecture is used as the basis of most modern LLMs.
- Transformers can be trained on system logs, network traffic and user behaviour to detect anomalous activity by detecting patterns over time and assets, revealing tactics such as C2 and lateral movement.
