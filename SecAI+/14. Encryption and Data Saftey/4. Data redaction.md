# Data redaction

- The process of removing or obscuring sensitive information.
- In AI systems, redaction protects privacy during input and output (e.g., PII).
- Prompts can be preprocessed to redact sensitive data.
  - Only the obfuscated version is used in the final prompt sent to the model.
- Post-processing can remove sensitive data from model outputs.
- Redaction ensures private details are obscured during retrieval in RAG systems.
- Understanding context is critical to successful data redaction.
  - Rule-based and AI models can help achieve this.
- Redacting data in images, video or audio files usually requires specialised tools.
