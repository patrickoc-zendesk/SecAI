# Data classification labelling

- Assigning labels to data based on levels of sensitivity, criticality and compliance requirements.
- These may include levels such as:
  - Public
  - Internal
  - Confidential
  - Secret
- Or types such as:
  - PII
  - Financial data
  - Health data
  - GDPR-regulated data
- Gives structure to how data is managed; determines how it is shared, accessed and what protections apply.
- Data classification should apply to training datasets, models and outputs.
- Benefits of data classification labelling include:
  - Helping to apply the correct security controls.
  - Assisting in regulatory compliance.
  - Enabling the correct management of data.
- AI can also be used to help assign data classification labels.
