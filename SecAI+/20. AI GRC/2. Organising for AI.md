# Organising for AI

- Organisational structures formalise decision-making authority
- Many organisations start with an AI Centre of Excellence (CoE) that helps them manage their AI portfolio
- AI adoption may use a hub-and-spoke model:
  - The CoE is the hub
  - The product and business units act as spokes that execute delivery
  - The spokes build and run their own solutions but rely on the hub for standard components, security controls, and approved workflows
- AI handoffs involve:
  - Product owners
  - Engineering teams
  - Risk, security, legal
  - Executive sponsors
