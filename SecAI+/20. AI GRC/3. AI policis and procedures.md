# AI policies and procedures

- Provides a common language for control and clarity
- It states intent, creates authority and sets scope
- Create an AI framework:
  - Policies set non-negotiable rules and intent for the whole organisation
    - They state what is permitted or prohibited, who it applies to and who is accountable
  - Standards convert policies into specific testable control statements that set the minimum bar across teams
  - Procedures describe how people complete a task step by step so that results stay consistent across teams and time
  - Guidelines offer recommended practices that help teams make good choices when a single rule would be too rigid
