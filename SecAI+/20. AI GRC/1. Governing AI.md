# Governing AI

- AI governance sets the rules around AI usage
- It protects people and data
- Reduces bias and leakage
- Improves reliability
- Prevents surprises
- Supports explainability
- Provides a record
- Common frameworks: NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF) and ISO/IEC 42001 (AI management systems)
- Governance spans the full AI lifecycle: design, development, deployment and monitoring
