# Excessive agency

- Occurs when teams improperly grant an AI system too much autonomy
- Driven by excessive functionality, excessive permissions or excessive autonomy (ability to act without approval)
- May improve efficiency, but helpful automation can lead to over-delegation
- Bots may make mistakes leading to security risks and financial problems
- Example: an agent with write/delete access to email or files takes damaging actions from a manipulated prompt
- Maps to OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications (Excessive Agency)
- Mitigations:
  - Least privilege on tools, permissions and functionality
  - Require human intervention or approval before an AI system can take high-risk actions
  - Limit the tools and scope exposed to the agent
