# AI supply chain attacks

- AI systems often depend on external components
  - Pre-trained models
  - Open-source libraries
  - Cloud services
  - Data feeds
  - 3rd party APIs
- Each introduces new risk
- Examples:
  - Malicious dependencies
  - Tampering with pre-trained models
    - Compromise of the foundation model
    - This can introduce a backdoor or introduce poisoned weights
  - Manipulating training data
    - In open data settings, contributing corrupted or mislabelled samples to a public dataset
  - Compromise of 3rd party services
- Maps to OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications (Supply Chain vulnerabilities)
- Mitigations:
  - Verify model and dataset provenance (signing, checksums, model cards)
  - Vet and pin dependencies; scan for known vulnerabilities
  - Maintain a software/AI bill of materials (SBOM/AI-BOM)
  - Source models and data only from trusted repositories
