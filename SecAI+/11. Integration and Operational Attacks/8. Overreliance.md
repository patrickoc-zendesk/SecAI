# Overreliance

- Occurs when users or systems place too much confidence in AI results
- Risk of acting on inaccurate, biased or hallucinated output without verification
- Human review should verify output before it is trusted
- Hiding uncertainty leads to overreliance
- Add friction to sensitive actions to force human input
- Surface uncertainty and rationale to the user
- Provide users with the context needed to challenge an output
- Maps to OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications (Overreliance)
- Trust has value, but unquestioning trust creates risk
