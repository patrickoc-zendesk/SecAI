# Membership inference

- An attempt at determining if a record was in the model's training set.
- This is important when the training data includes sensitive or private information.
- This attack exploits the differences in how a model sees familiar vs unfamiliar data.
- Overfitted models are more vulnerable, as they are more confident on data they were trained on.
- There are two main vectors for this attack:
  - White box: attacks include access to model internals.
  - Black box: attacks only observe model output.

## Mitigation

- Reduce overfitting through regularisation.
- Apply differential privacy during training.
- Limit confidence scores exposed in outputs.
