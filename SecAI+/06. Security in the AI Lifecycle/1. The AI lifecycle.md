# The AI lifecycle

- The AI lifecycle describes the stages a model moves through from initial planning to retirement.

1. Planning and Business Alignment
   - Define objectives for the AI
   - Define how to measure success
2. Data Collection
3. Data Preparation
4. Model Selection and Development
5. Model Evaluation and Validation
6. Model Deployment and Integration
7. Monitoring and Maintenance
8. Feedback and Iteration

- The lifecycle is cyclical, with the feedback stage informing the next planning and business alignment stage.
- Security should be embedded at every stage rather than added at the end (security by design).
