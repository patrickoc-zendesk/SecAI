# Data collection

- High-quality data is the foundation of every AI system.
- The reliability of a model is determined by the quality, diversity and trustworthiness of the data.
- During data collection, data should first be reviewed for accuracy, completeness and authenticity.
- It should also be checked for representativeness, provenance, bias, and fairness.
- Legal and ethical factors are also crucial during data collection.
- Data minimisation is a key principle in data collection:
  - Only gather what is necessary to achieve the project's goals.
  - Collecting extra data only increases cost and risk.
- Diversity of the data is also crucial:
  - Models trained on narrow examples perform poorly in new situations.
- Untrusted or unvetted sources introduce the risk of data poisoning, where malicious samples corrupt the trained model.
