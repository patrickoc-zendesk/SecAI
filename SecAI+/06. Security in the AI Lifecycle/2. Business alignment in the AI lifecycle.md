# Business alignment in the AI lifecycle

- This stage focuses on:
  - Defining what problem the new tool/feature/model will solve
  - Why it matters
  - Defining what success looks like
- Without this focus, the model may produce results which do not support the company's goals.
- During this stage, teams define the AI business case and expected value.
- A clear understanding of the business will help define the system boundaries.
- Risk assessment can help identify what can go wrong if the system fails or is compromised.
- This is also the stage to set measurable success criteria (KPIs) and acceptable risk thresholds.
