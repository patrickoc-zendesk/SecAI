# Supervised learning

- Supervised learning models are trained on a labelled dataset.
  - Every example in the training data contains the correct answer.
  - Predictions are based on the labelled correct answers.
  - Supervised learning is used for classification and regression tasks.
- Classification focuses on predicting a categorical value.
  - Example: determining if an email is spam or not.
- Regression focuses on predicting a continuous value.
  - Example: forecasting how many attacks a network will face over a period of time.
- Limitations include:
  - High time cost required to label the training data.
  - Poor quality labels can produce unreliable results.
  - Struggles with new or unseen data, resulting in incorrect classification.
