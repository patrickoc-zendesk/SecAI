# Fine tuning

- A technique used to adapt models for specific tasks.
- An existing trained model is retrained on a smaller, task specific dataset in a process known as transfer learning.
- This helps models better understand unique threat environments.
- Fine-tuning also addresses overfitting and concept drift.
- Too few training cycles (or epochs) and the model will not learn enough, while too many will overfit the data.
- Advanced fine-tuning techniques are used to prevent overfitting:
  - Pruning
    - Reduces model complexity in a neural network by removing weights/neurons that contribute little to performance.
  - Quantisation
    - Improves efficiency by lowering the precision of the numerical values used in computations, making the model smaller and faster while maintaining nearly the same level of accuracy.
