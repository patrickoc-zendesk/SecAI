# Data poisoning

- Data poisoning attacks tamper with training data
- An attacker manipulates the data used to teach an AI
- They inject malicious or misleading information into that data to influence how the model learns and behaves
- Especially dangerous when public or user-supplied data is collected for training
- The goal is to change the model's internal logic, causing it to make incorrect associations and decisions
- Attackers may insert backdoors into a model this way
- Even a small portion of poisoned training data can influence the model
- Mitigations: validate and sanitise data sources, track data provenance, use anomaly detection on training sets, and control who can contribute data
