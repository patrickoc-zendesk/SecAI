# Log monitoring

- Expands visibility by collecting and analysing logs from every component that supports the AI service
- Log reviews help verify system performance and the effectiveness of security controls
- Most companies rely on centralised platforms such as `SIEM` or `XDR` to manage log data
- Key practices:
  - Establish uniform log formatting
  - Ensure log sanitisation
  - Employ log protection
  - External audit logging services ensure logs are protected even in the event of a compromise
  - Establish clear log retention policies
- `SIEM` (Security Information and Event Management) aggregates and correlates logs; `XDR` (Extended Detection and Response) adds automated cross-layer detection and response
