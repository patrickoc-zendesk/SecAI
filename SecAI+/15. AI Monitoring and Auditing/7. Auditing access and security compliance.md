# Auditing access and security compliance

- Confirms that safeguards are both present and effective by ensuring that AI systems protect sensitive data and operate within established governance and regulatory standards
- Verifies that access controls, authentication methods, and data protection measures work as intended, and that every system action can be traced to an authorised user
- The goal is to confirm that safeguards are present and effective
- Practices include:
  - Establish clear governance:
    - Define policies that specify who can access AI systems, how access is approved, and when it should be revoked
    - This establishes accountability and ensures that permissions are reviewed regularly
  - Audit access controls
    - Follow the principle of least privilege and ensure that critical tasks are separated among multiple roles
    - No individual should have full authority to both initiate and approve sensitive actions (separation of duties)
  - Maintain audit trails
    - Record major actions such as logins, access and updates
    - Ensure that they map correctly to users
  - Verify regulatory compliance
    - Data privacy frameworks such as `GDPR` (EU) require that organisations maintain detailed records of data access, including timestamps, user IDs and the purpose of each action
  - Evaluate data protection measures
    - Review logs, models and sensitive datasets to ensure that they are encrypted both at rest and in transit
    - Ensure that there is tamper protection and that keys are rotated
  - Monitor third-party access
