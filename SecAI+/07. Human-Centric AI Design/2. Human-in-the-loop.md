# Human-in-the-loop

- A human-centric design approach where a human stays actively involved in the AI's decision-making process
- Humans provide oversight and context that an AI may not fully understand
- HitL systems are especially valuable in high-risk environments (e.g. medical, financial, or safety-critical decisions)
- The human can approve, reject, or amend an AI recommendation before it takes effect
- Contrast: human-on-the-loop (monitors and can intervene) vs human-out-of-the-loop (fully autonomous)
- Reduces risk of acting on flawed or manipulated model outputs
