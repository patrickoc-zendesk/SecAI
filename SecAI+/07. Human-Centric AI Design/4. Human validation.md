# Human validation

- Focuses on what happens after an AI has made a decision
- An audit-style action; human validation ensures AI models continue to perform as intended
- Periodic validation ensures AI outputs stay aligned with organisational goals and policy
- Helps identify model drift and data drift
- Example: sampling and reviewing a batch of model outputs against ground truth on a set schedule
- Findings feed back into retraining, tuning, or rollback decisions
