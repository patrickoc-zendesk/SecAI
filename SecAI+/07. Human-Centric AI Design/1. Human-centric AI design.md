# Human-centric AI design

- A design philosophy that works for people, not around them
- Supports human decision-making rather than replacing it
- Promotes transparency by making AI reasoning clear so users can challenge outputs
- Aims to ensure ethical and secure outcomes
- Keeps humans accountable for decisions the AI supports
- Related patterns: human-in-the-loop, human oversight, and human validation
