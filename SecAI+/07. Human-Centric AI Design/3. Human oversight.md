# Human oversight

- Instead of reviewing each decision, this approach maintains visibility over decisions and performance
- Dashboards, alert systems, and controls let humans track and correct AI behaviour in real time
- Transparency features such as explainable outputs and accessible logs enable human oversight
- Ensures humans stay in command of outcomes even when the AI is making the decisions
- Supports intervention (pause, override, or roll back) when anomalous behaviour is detected
