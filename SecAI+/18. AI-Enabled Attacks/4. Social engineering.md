# Social engineering

- GenAI enhances the capabilities of phishing and other social engineering attacks with minimal effort
- This includes spear phishing, voice cloning, and fake chatbots impersonating known brands and sites
- Lowers the barrier to entry: fluent, personalised lures at scale, without the spelling and grammar tells of older phishing
- Can translate and localise attacks to target victims in any language
- Mitigations: user awareness training, phishing-resistant MFA, and out-of-band verification of unusual requests
