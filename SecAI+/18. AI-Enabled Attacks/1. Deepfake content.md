# Deepfake content

- Deepfakes are highly realistic, AI-generated or manipulated media that depict people saying or doing things they never did, or fabricate scenes with convincing detail
- Malicious parties use deepfakes as part of phishing, fraud and influence operations
- Example: a cloned executive voice or video used to authorise a fraudulent payment (business email/voice compromise)
- Disinformation spreads when people knowingly share false content
  - This has become easier through the use of LLMs to draft persuasive narratives, image and video generators to supply synthetic visuals, and social media bots that push the material to carefully chosen audiences who may share it unwittingly
- Mitigations: media provenance and watermarking, detection tools, out-of-band verification of unusual requests, and user awareness training
