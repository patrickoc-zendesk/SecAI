# Adversarial networks

- Attackers exploit generative adversarial network (`GAN`) training loops to produce content that defeats both human scrutiny and automated defences
- A GAN pairs a generator against a discriminator; the two compete until the generated output is hard to distinguish from real data
- Attackers iteratively refine images, audio, or text so that the outputs are missed by filters, poison downstream models, or mislead decision-making systems
- Targets include:
  - Content moderation
  - Deepfake detectors
  - Spam filters
- Defending requires robust training pipelines that include adversarial examples, the use of ensemble techniques that combine multiple models, and continuous red-team testing
