# Data integrity

- Data integrity involves protecting information from unauthorised alteration
- Ensure that data is consistent, accurate and reliable for its entire lifecycle
- In the context of AI, small changes can have large effects on model behaviour
- Safeguarding integrity can be achieved through digital signatures, cryptographic hashes, and version control systems that track modification.
- Access control can also be used to limit `CRUD` (create, read, update, delete) operations
- Data integrity also includes accountability
- One of the three pillars of the CIA triad (confidentiality, integrity, availability).
