# Data lineage and provenance

- Data lineage documents the full path the data takes from source to final use
  - This includes every transformation, aggregation and processing step that occurs
- This visibility allows for:
  - Error tracing
  - Result explanation
  - Reproduction of past experiments
- Data provenance documents the origin and authenticity of data
- It answers the questions of:
  - Who created the data?
  - Where was it first collected?
  - Does it come from a reliable source?
- Very important for regulated industries
- Together, lineage and provenance strengthen transparency and accountability
- Helps detect supply-chain risks such as poisoned or unlicensed data entering the training set.
