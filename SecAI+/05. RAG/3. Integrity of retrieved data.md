# Integrity of retrieved data

- The reliability of a RAG system depends on the quality of the data it retrieves.
- Outdated, inaccurate or maliciously modified data will produce false or harmful outputs.
- This issue is known as a retrieval integrity issue.
- Maintaining data integrity in RAG requires verifying both the source and the content of each document added to the knowledge store.
- This includes:
  - Checking the authenticity of new entries.
    - Digital signatures or hash values.
  - Validating the trustworthiness of data sources.
  - Implementing human review of new content.
- Related threat: data poisoning, where an attacker seeds the knowledge store with malicious content to steer model outputs.
