# Change management

- Change management is the structured process for modifications to systems, applications, or configurations
- AI supports faster approvals and safe rollouts by:
  - Identifying risks
  - Creating impact assessments
  - Proposing changes and generating recommendations
- Benefit: reduces manual effort and speeds up review of routine changes
- Risk: AI recommendations still need human approval and an audit trail; retain accountability for change decisions
