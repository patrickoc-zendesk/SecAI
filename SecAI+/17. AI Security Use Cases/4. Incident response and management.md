# Incident response and management

- AI can help by
  - Organising information
  - Suggesting actions
  - Collecting alerts
  - Grouping related events
  - Summarising timelines
  - Automating responses
  - Drafting communications
- AI improves the speed and efficiency of incident response
- Reduces analyst fatigue by triaging and prioritising high volumes of alerts
- Automated actions should follow approved playbooks with human oversight for high-impact steps
